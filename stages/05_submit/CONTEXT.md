# 05_submit - hand it in

One job: fill the submission form with everything the brief asks for.

## Inputs
- Working (this run): `../04_deploy/output/live-url.md`
- Working (this run): `output/loom-script.md`
- Reference (every run): `../../_shared/brief.md` (deliverables list)
- Deliverables: `../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `../../docs/FAILURE_TEST.md`, `../../docs/THESIS.md`, `../../docs/NOTES.md`

Do NOT load: `app/` source, tests.

## Process
1. Record the 90-second video following `output/loom-script.md`.
2. Confirm `docs/THESIS.md` is ≤300 words.
3. Submit: repo URL, live URL, video URL, and paste `docs/NOTES.md` into the Notes field.

## Outputs
- `submitted.md` -> `output/` (date, URLs)

## Check
A person ticks every line of the brief's submission checklist in `output/submitted.md`.
